# Dinner Time: Seating and Drink Lines

Lines for the new service steps: **seating** (step 2), **drink order** (step 3), and **bringing the drink** (step 4). These are quick small-talk beats with no player choices. The big choices stay at the food order. Pick one line at random per stage, and avoid repeats using seen_lines.

Tokens: [name], [nickname], [drink] (the drink they order).

Usual orders: each regular orders their favorite drink most of the time (about 70%) and something else the rest.

| Regular | Favorite | Orders before favorite is unlocked |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Mabel | black_tea | (always unlocked) |
| Stitches | hot_chocolate | (always unlocked) |
| Cheddar | bubble_tea | koala_cola |

## Stitches

### Seating

| Stage | Lines |
| --- | --- |
| Nervous | "Is the corner one free? ...Only if it's okay." / "I can sit anywhere. Well. The corner, if I could. Sorry." |
| Warming | "The corner, please. It's kind of my table now. Is that weird?" |
| Opening up | "My corner! You kept it free. ...You didn't keep it free. But it feels like you did." |
| Confident | "Can I try a middle table today? ...Okay. Okay! I'm doing it." (He alternates between the corner and the middle from now on.) |
| Confused / Hurt | "The corner's fine." He sits before you finish leading him. |

### Drink order

| Stage | Lines |
| --- | --- |
| Nervous | "Um... could I have a hot chocolate? If it's not too much trouble." / "Just water is fine. ...Actually, could I have a hot chocolate? Sorry. Hot chocolate." |
| Warming | "Hot chocolate, please. With a marshmallow, if you have one. I won't be upset if you don't." / "I'll try a [drink] today. Being brave." |
| Opening up | "Hot chocolate, please, [name]. The marshmallow is my favorite part. I save it for last." / "Do you have a favorite drink? ...I want to try it." (He orders whatever the player's last-served drink was.) |
| Confident | "Hot chocolate. Two marshmallows. I'm a two-marshmallow bear now." / "Surprise me with a drink, too! I like surprises now." (Player picks the drink.) |
| Confused | "Water, please. I don't need anything special." |
| Hurt | "Water." |

### Bringing the drink

- Favorite, Nervous or Warming: "You brought a marshmallow. ...Thank you. Really."
- Favorite, Opening up or Confident: "It's perfect. You always make it perfect, [name]."
- Something else: "Thank you. It's really nice." (He'd say this about anything.)
- Confused or Hurt: "Thank you." He doesn't look up.

## Cheddar

### Seating

| Stage | Lines |
| --- | --- |
| Showman | "New table today. Gotta keep you guessing." / "I'll sit... THERE. No wait. There. Final answer." |
| Sidekick | "Table for one very famous mouse, please. Lead the way, [nickname]." |
| Real talk | "I'll take the one closest to the kitchen. ...For the smell. Not you. Mostly the smell." |
| Best friend | "Usual table, partner. Wait, I don't have a usual table. ...Pick one for me. That's my usual now." |
| Snarky / Cold | "I can find a seat myself." |

### Drink order

| Stage | Lines |
| --- | --- |
| Showman | "One Koala-Cola, extra bubbles. Can you add extra bubbles? Try." / "What's the loudest drink you've got? I'll have that." (He orders whichever bold soda is unlocked.) |
| Sidekick | "Bubble tea! The pearls are like a snack inside a drink. Genius." / "Okay, drink bit: I describe it, you guess. It's brown and fizzy and I love it. ...It's Koala-Cola. You got it, you're amazing." |
| Real talk | "Bubble tea, please. ...Actually, you know what? I'll have whatever you're having. I wanna know what you like." |
| Best friend | "The usual, partner. Bubble tea, extra pearls, and a straw I can make noises with." / "Surprise me! A drink AND a meal. Double mystery." (Player picks the drink.) |
| Snarky | "Koala-Cola. Can you handle that, or is it too complicated?" |
| Cold | "Water." |

### Bringing the drink

- Favorite: He slurps the pearls loudly on purpose. "Ah. Perfection. Five stars."
- A soda: "Fizzy! You get me." Sometimes he shakes it first, and it sprays him. "...Worth it."
- Something else: "Ooh, a mystery drink! I'll allow it."
- Snarky or Cold: "Fine."

## Mabel

### Seating

| Stage | Lines |
| --- | --- |
| Inspector | "The window table. I like to watch the door. Old habit." / She runs a claw along the table before sitting and inspects it. |
| Regular | "My window, please, [name]. And don't rush me, I'll get there." |
| Storyteller | "Window table. Walter and I always sat by the window, you know." |
| Family | "I can find my own table, dear. I've been finding it for months." She heads there herself, slowly, waving you off. |
| Disappointed / Formal | "Any table is fine, chef." |

### Drink order

| Stage | Lines |
| --- | --- |
| Inspector | "Black tea. Strong. Don't drown it in milk." / "What tea do you use? ...Hm. Fine. Black tea." |
| Regular | "Black tea, please, [name]. Leave the bag in. I'll tell you when it's done." / "Just water today. I'm watching my figure. ...I'm joking, dear. Black tea." |
| Storyteller | "Black tea. Walter drank his coffee so strong you could stand a spoon in it. I never understood it." |
| Family | "You know my order, dear. You've known it for months." (She doesn't say it. The player brings the tea, and she gives a small nod if it's right.) / "Let me try one of your fancy drinks today. Not the green one. ...Fine, the green one." (She orders the matcha latte if it's unlocked.) |
| Disappointed | "Black tea, please." |
| Formal | "Tea. Thank you, chef." |

### Bringing the drink

- Favorite, Inspector: She smells it, sips, and nods once. "Acceptable."
- Favorite, later stages: "Just right. You've learned."
- Something else (when she asked for tea): "This isn't what I ordered, dear. ...I'll drink it anyway. I don't waste."
- Matcha latte at Family: A long, suspicious sip. "...It's green. It's good. I'm not saying it twice."
- Disappointed or Formal: "Thank you."
