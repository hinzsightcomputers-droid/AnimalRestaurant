# Split-screen performance, 2026-11-12

Measured with `tools/perf_check.tscn` (see the top of `tools/perf_check.gd` for how to run it).

- Machine: the dev laptop, NVIDIA GeForce RTX 4060 Laptop GPU.
- Window: 1920x1080, toon style on.
- Vsync off, so these are headroom numbers, not the monitor's cap.
- Save: day 8, breakfast opening, with a few customers in line.
- Chefs spread round the room: dining room, kitchen, back of the dining room, host stand.
- 12 seconds per player count.

| Players | Avg fps | 1% low fps | Worst frame | Draw calls | Triangles |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 543 | 292 | 7.5 ms | 300 | 205k |
| 2 | 354 | 175 | 24 ms | 547 | 385k |
| 3 | 197 | 114 | 11 ms | 1,602 | 1.09M |
| 4 | 163 | 85 | 19 ms | 1,632 | 1.21M |

## What it found

- **Plenty of headroom on this laptop.** Even four players stay well above 60 fps, with 1% lows of 85 fps.
- **The three-player "overview" quarter was the most expensive view.** It drew the whole grounds and the forest, and cost more than a fourth player's view:
  - Before: 2,354 draw calls, 164 fps at three players, against 1,589 draw calls and 175 fps at four.
  - Change: it now redraws every third frame (`ViewManager.OVERVIEW_EVERY`).
  - After: 1,602 draw calls, 197 fps at three players.
- **The overview now matches the new view.** It used to look from the old front angle. It now uses CameraRig.VIEW_YAW, keeps the front wall low, and hides the side wall it looks in over.
- **Split screen roughly doubles triangle counts at three or four players, and that's mostly the trees.** With four views, each one sees part of the forest.
  - If a low-end PC struggles, cheaper trees are the first place to look: lower-detail far trees, or culling the forest past the yard.
- **Worst-frame spikes of 19–24 ms show up when a player joins.** That is the chef being built and the account screens. They aren't seen mid-service.

## Not covered

- **A low-end PC.** This is the "Performance pass on a low-end PC" roadmap task. Rule of thumb: integrated graphics run roughly 5–10x slower than this GPU, so the four-player toon view could land near 20–30 fps there. That's worth testing before the beta.
- **A full lunch rush with every table eating.** This run had 2–6 customers.
