## What it found

1. **No second star all week: decor stalls at 20 of 25.** Service, menu and
   community were all done by day 5 (menu 21 of 18, community 95 of 50).
   At 1 star the shop only has 7 decor pieces (tier 1). One of each places
   about 16–20 points, so 25 needs second copies (max_copies is 2). The bot
   buys one of each, so it never gets there, and a new player probably
   wouldn't guess that duplicates are the way either. The plan was star 2
   around the end of week 1 (edca73d). To decide: lower the decor bar for
   star 2, add a few more tier-1 pieces, or have the Rating page say "a second
   lamp counts too".
2. **Money piles up with nothing to spend it on.** About $440–530 earned
   a day (about half of it tips), and $1,873 unspent by the end of day 7. From
   day 4, each shop visit buys only 3–4 things (all that's in stock) and
   leaves more money than the day before. This feeds the money-pacing task:
   either more to buy at 1 star, a costlier upgrade or two, or lower pay.
   A real player earns less than the bot, so check against a human day too.
3. **Washing up is the slowest step: 12–19 s a time** (11 a day), with 1
   dish left over for a while. The second slowest is plating the big
   breakfast (about 11 s). Worth checking by hand whether the last dish is
   fiddly or it's only the bot.
4. **Walk-in lines no longer repeat much.** At most 3 times a day (the
   3-line sets), down from 9 before today's fix (fe4e434).
5. **Small things:** the end-of-day screen with Friends / Rating /
   Collections / Shop pauses the bot for a moment (harmless). On day 6 the
   night shift sat 90 s with no phone orders before "The phone has gone
   quiet", which was a quiet stretch, not something stuck.
