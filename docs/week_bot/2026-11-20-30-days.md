## Problems

1. **Customers run out mid-meal (day 1, and in co-op).** Walk-ins come only from
   townsfolk who haven't visited today; once all have, nobody comes while the HUD
   still counts "next in 2s". Solo day 1 had 90 s of empty dinner rush; 4 chefs
   had up to ~4.5 minutes of nobody. Not seen solo after day 1. Design call needed.
2. **Days run past the bot's 30 minutes (bot limit, but worth a look).** From day
   4, 22 of 27 days were still serving the closing tables at 30 game minutes, so
   the bot never reached the summary or shop those days. Finished days reach the
   summary at 28–30 minutes. A real player would get there, but the closing tail
   (last tables' drinks, orders and bills) is long once the restaurant is busy.
3. **The money pile-up is partly the bot.** It shops only on days it reaches the
   summary (days 8, 13, 20, 23, 26, 30), so savings look bigger than a real
   player's. But the shop also runs dry: the menu stops at 36 on day 13, and on
   day 30 there was nothing to buy with $11,475. Restaurant stages (still
   placeholder) are the obvious place for that money.
4. **Washing's last dishes are slow.** 49 slow steps in 30 days, all "Wash the
   dishes", most often stuck on the last dish. Could be the bot's scrubbing
   pattern; worth trying by hand.
5. **4 chefs crowd the second stove.** A few times a day a chef couldn't get it
   highlighted, most likely another chef in the way. Only with 4 players.

## Fixed (2026-11-20)

1. Once everyone in town has been in, whoever has been in least comes back
   (most_visits_each no longer shuts the doors). Retest, 4 chefs day 1: no empty
   spells (was 3), 45 customers served.
2. Dinner arrivals stop 100 s before the end (was 60). Retest day 8: summary at
   29.8 game minutes (was 29.9): little change on a day that already finished.
3. The bot now pays for the next renovation; it bought Cozy Bistro on day 8.
   Stages 3-5 are still "Coming in a later update".
4. Bins of 4+ dishes have one grime spot fewer per dish.
5. The Guide sends a chef to a free stove or chopping board (it used to send
   everyone to the nearest stove and always the first board). Retest: no
   "can't reach" (was 2).
